Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 29, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information ClipAja ("the App") collects, how it is used, and the choices you have. ClipAja is a personal web tool for creating short clips from your own videos and publishing them to your TikTok account.

1. Information we collect

When you sign in with TikTok, the App receives the information you authorize through TikTok Login Kit, such as your TikTok open ID, display name, avatar, and an access token that allows the App to publish content on your behalf. The App also processes the video files you upload or select so it can create and publish clips.

2. How we use your information

Your information is used only to operate the App: to sign you in, to show which account you are posting to, to create clips from your videos, and to publish clips to TikTok when you confirm the upload. We do not use your information for advertising.

3. Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties. Your videos and post details are sent only to TikTok, through TikTok's official API, and only when you choose to publish.

4. Storage and retention

Access tokens are stored securely and used only to perform actions you request. Uploaded videos and generated clips are stored temporarily for processing and are deleted after publishing or within a short period. You can ask us to delete your data at any time.

5. Your choices

You can disconnect the App from your TikTok account at any time in TikTok's settings under security and permissions. You can also request deletion of any data held by the App by contacting us using the email below.

6. Security

We use reasonable measures, such as HTTPS and restricted access to stored tokens, to protect your information. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we work to keep your data safe.

7. Children

The App is not intended for anyone under the minimum age required to use TikTok in their country.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The date at the top of this page shows when it was last changed.

9. Contact

For privacy questions or data deletion requests, contact YOUR_EMAIL@example.com.

ClipAja

Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 29, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information ClipAja ("the App") uses, how it is used, and the choices you have. ClipAja is a personal desktop tool that runs on your own computer. It creates short clips from videos you own or have permission to use, and publishes them to your TikTok accounts only when you confirm each post.

1. Information the App uses

When you connect a TikTok account through TikTok Login Kit, the App receives the information you authorize: your TikTok open ID, username, display name, and an access token and refresh token that allow the App to publish content you confirm. Your avatar is shown in the App while you post but is not stored.

The App also processes the video files you select or the video links you provide, so it can transcribe them, create clips, and publish the clips you choose.

2. How your information is used

Your information is used only to operate the App: to connect your TikTok accounts, to show which account you are posting to, to create clips from your videos, and to publish clips to TikTok when you confirm the upload. We do not use your information for advertising.

3. Services the App communicates with

Your videos, captions, and post settings are sent to TikTok through TikTok's official API, and only when you choose to publish.

To choose clip moments and draft captions, the text transcript of a video is sent to an AI language model provider (such as OpenRouter). Your TikTok account information and tokens are never sent to it.

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with anyone else.

4. Storage and retention

All data stays on your own computer. Access tokens and refresh tokens are stored encrypted on your computer. Videos, transcripts, and generated clips are stored in the App's folder on your computer until you delete them. The App does not operate its own server and does not keep copies of your data anywhere else.

5. Your choices and deleting your data

You can disconnect a TikTok account at any time using the "Disconnect" option in the App, which also asks TikTok to revoke the App's access and removes the account's tokens from your computer. You can also remove the App's access in TikTok's settings under security and app permissions.

Because all data is stored on your own computer, you can delete it completely at any time by deleting the App's folder. For questions, contact us using the email below.

6. Security

Access tokens are encrypted on your computer using your operating system account's encryption, and all communication with TikTok uses HTTPS. The App's own interface runs only on your computer and cannot be reached from the internet. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, but we work to keep your data safe.

7. Children

The App is not intended for anyone under the minimum age required to use TikTok in their country.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The date at the top of this page shows when it was last changed.
